Sosyal Panel

Sosyal Panel is a personal analytics dashboard. It lets the owner view performance statistics of their own social media content accounts (such as views, likes, comments and shares) in one place, so they can understand which content works best.

The tool is used only by its owner, BATIKAN KARACA, for accounts the owner personally controls. It is not offered to the public.
