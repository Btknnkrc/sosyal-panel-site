Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Who we are

Sosyal Panel is a personal analytics tool operated by BATIKAN KARACA ("the owner"). It is used only by the owner to analyze the performance of social media accounts that the owner personally controls.

What data we access

When the owner connects a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the tool may access, with the owner's permission: basic profile information, profile statistics (such as follower, like and video counts), and the list of the account's public videos with their statistics (such as views, likes, comments and shares).

How we use the data

The data is used only to display performance statistics and to generate summaries and recommendations for the owner. Summarized statistics may be processed by an AI service to produce written analysis for the owner.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored in a private database on the owner's own computer. We do not sell, rent, publish or share TikTok data with any third party for advertising or any other purpose.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as the owner uses the tool and can be deleted at any time by the owner. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services (or Manage app permissions). After revocation, no further data is collected.
